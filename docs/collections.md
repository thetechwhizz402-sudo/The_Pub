# Game Collections

The [Collections page](https://lewdpub.com/collections) brings related game listings together into curated or automatically organized groups.

The public page includes discovery groupings such as trending titles, completed games, and top-rated games. A collection can help visitors explore a particular subset of the catalog without manually applying several filters.

## When to use collections

Collections are useful when you want to browse by a broad theme or status rather than search for one specific title. They can also provide a starting point for discovering games you may not have encountered through the homepage.

Collection membership and counts can change as listings are added or updated. See [LewdPub Collections](https://lewdpub.com/collections) for the current groups.
